      How SoundSlate Empowers Non-Visual Learning

      SoundSlate eliminates visual learning barriers by acting as an intelligent acoustic interpreter between complex statistical figures and non-visual cognitive understanding.

      timeline 1. Tactile & Spatial Translation: Charts are parsed into structural coordinates rather than generic alt-text, delivering precise axes, ranges, and legends in an organized hierarchy.
      equalizer 2. Continuous Acoustic Sonification: Audio oscillators modulate frequencies linearly according to bar heights and trend lines, enabling rapid mental graph comprehension without reading thousands of words.
      record_voice_over 3. Conversational Socratic Dialogue: Ask spoken follow-up inquiries to interrogate specific years, calculate percentage changes, check outliers, or generate practice quizzes.
